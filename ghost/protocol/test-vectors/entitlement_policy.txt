# Entitlement client policy vectors (Phase 8 design §11.9, §13.4 "reference policy", §12.3–§12.5,
# §19.4, §19.8, §19.11, §19.14). The pure decisions of the client's schedule: redemption planning,
# slot eligibility (activation slots), quiet-run work selection and retries. One file, two replayers:
#   - ghost/android/entitlement/src/test/kotlin/org/ghost/entitlement/harness/PolicyVectorsTest.kt
#     replays it against the real Kotlin engine (RedeemPlanner, ClockEstimate, Slots, RetryPolicy,
#     QuietRunWork, Pricing);
#   - the Rust T2 reference policy (slice S10) replays it against its mirror of those decisions.
# A decision that is not pinned here is not one the reference policy may assume.
#
# GRAMMAR
# - One operation per line. '#' starts a comment (to the end of the line). Blank lines are ignored.
# - Words are separated by spaces; the first word is the operation, then key=value arguments (the
#   first argument of `classify` is a category word). "-> <expectation>" ends a line with an outcome.
#
# Values
#   <time>     <week>+<s> or <week>-<s>: start(week) ± s seconds, start(p) = 345600 + 604800 p
#              (Monday 00:00 UTC). Week 2975 starts Monday 2027-01-11.
#   <onion>    <label>:<port>: the onion whose service key is SHA-256(label) (as in redeem.txt), at
#              that port.
#   <u>        a draw in [0, 1): a PRF output or a uniform the decision consumes (in order).
#   none       no value.
#
# Operations
#   slot <n> onion=<onion> from=<week> until=<week>|open
#       adds an entry to the slot table of the file (ES slot n served by that onion from week `from`,
#       until `until` exclusive). Every later line uses the table built so far.
#   boundary t=<time> -> yes|no
#       the ±1 h redeem guard (§12.4): t lies within one hour of a week boundary.
#   slotsfor relay=<onion> week=<w> -> <n>,<n>…|none
#       the ES slots the relay serves in week w (§19.22 point 3): those listed under its exact
#       onion:port, otherwise the single slot listed under its service key; a key holding several
#       slots in the week with an unlisted port serves none.
#   plan kind=write|read reason=missing|expiring|exhausted|rejected relay=<onion> now=<time>
#        week=<w> trusted=yes|no first=<time> write_expiry=<time>|none prf=<u>
#       -> redeem week=<w> slots=<list> due=<time> | deferred | no_slot | skip
#       one need (§12.4): now is the relay-facing estimate, week the relay's week, first the first
#       sighting of the need in this process, write_expiry the expiry of the pair's usable write
#       capability, prf the need's PRF draw. Deferred without a trusted clock or within ±1 h of a
#       boundary; EXPIRING in the last 23 h of week p targets p + 1 at start(p+1) − 23 h + prf × 22 h;
#       READ MISSING is due at first + prf × 6 h; everything else at once; skipped when the write
#       capability reaches start(target + 1); no_slot when the relay serves no slot of the target week.
#       Due times truncate the PRF's fraction of the window to whole seconds.
#   estimate reset | estimate record relay=<id> relay_minute=<m> local=<time> period=<w> wrong=yes|no
#   estimate now wall=<time> -> <time> | estimate week relay=<id> wall=<time> -> <w>
#       the relay-facing clock (§12.5, §19.4): each relay's latest offset relay_minute − local minute,
#       clipped to ±24 h; with ≥ 2 relays the median (the lower-rounded mean of the two middle ones
#       for an even count), else none; a WRONG_PERIOD answer's period is adopted for that relay while
#       it names a week within 24 h of the wall clock, and any other answer drops the adoption.
#   eligible batch=pack|trial mode=standard|high finalized=<time> uniforms=<u>,…|none -> <time>
#       activation slots (§12.3): a pack's tokens at floor_minute(first UTC-day boundary ≥ t + 4 h
#       + U[0, 6 h)), plus Geometric(1/2) whole days in HIGH (a draw < 0.5 adds a day, at most 16;
#       the draws come first, then the offset's); a trial's at floor_minute(t) in STANDARD and by the
#       pack rule in HIGH.
#   attempt seed=<hex32> receipt=<time> k=<0..4> -> <time>
#       the due minute of BlindSign attempt k (§19.11): receipt + window_k low + u × window width,
#       rounded up to a minute, u the top 53 bits of HKDF-SHA256(ikm = seed, salt = none,
#       info = "ghost/v1/attempt" ‖ u8(k), 8 bytes); windows [3 h, 5 h], [44 h, 52 h],
#       [100 h, 112 h], [7 d, 8 d], [20 d, 22 d].
#   retry attempt=<n> current=<time>|none now=<time> uniform=<u> -> <time>|none
#       the due time written ahead with a capped call's send (§19.11): at the first send the one
#       retry at ceil_minute(now + 20 h + u × 8 h), afterwards the current one unchanged.
#   classify <category> -> transient|unauthorized|rejected|malformed
#       what a failed call means to its flow (§5.7).
#   work now=<time> items=<kind>@<time>,… -> <index>|none
#       quiet-run work (§11.6, §12.2, J9): the one issuer call of a quiet run is the most overdue due
#       item (due ≤ now), ties in the listed order; kinds request, sign, refresh, revocation, claim,
#       renewal. The answer is the item's 0-based position.
#   cover prices=<epoch>:<price>,… credits=<epoch>,… base=<week> now=<week> -> <index>,…|none
#       the credits a credits-paid pack sends (§4.6, §19.8): a credit is worth the price of its epoch
#       / 10 and is accepted in its epoch and the four following; the smallest set of at least 10 and
#       at most 20 accepted credits covering price(price_epoch(base)), taken highest value first, the
#       older epoch first among equal values, then in the listed order. The answer lists the chosen
#       credits' 0-based positions in the order taken.
